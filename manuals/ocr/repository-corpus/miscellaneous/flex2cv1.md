 FLEX2CV1 AND FLEX2CVA ARE CONVERISON DATA FOR FLEX MINI,1.0/2.0 & 09.

	WHEN YOU FIND A SOURCE FOR FLEX 1.0 OR 2.0 JUST CHANGE VECTORS
	FOR 09,NORMALLY CHANGE $AXXX TO $CXXX AND $BXXX TO $DXXX.

	MINIFLEX IS ANOTHER STORY,SEVERAL OTHER ITEMS MAY HAVE TO BE CHANGED*

	PLEASE NOTE MANY PROGRAMS WRITTEN MAY USE DIRECT VECTOR TO SWTBUG
	$E000-$E3FF. THESE VECTORS WILL NOT WORK WITH 09.

	PLEASE NOTE IO PORT VECTORS DIFFER IN FLEX..YOUR S09 SEEMS
	TO BE SUCH A CASE.FLEX 1,2,& 9 ARE LISTED BELOW,BUT I DO NOT KNOW
	THAT S09 VETCORS ARE IN FACT AS LISTED..

 #	1/2.0	09	S09
	SWT	EBUG FLEXS09 ?
 ============================================================

 1	8004 E004 E010 CONNTROL PORT

 3	800C E00C E030 CALULATOR
 4	8010 E010 E040 INTERRUPT TIMER
 5	8014 E014 E050 1/2 FOR MINI DISK CONTROLLER
 6	8018 E018 E060 MINI-CONTROLLER
 7	801C E01C E070 PRINTER PORT IN MOST SYSTEMS : EPROM (MISC) IN MINE.
