INFORMATION ON PT-69 FLEX DRIVERS	05/12/84

1) BOOT YOUR SYSTEM USING THE TSC MASTER DISK
2) PLACE THIS DRIVER DISKETTE IN DRIVE 1
3) ENTER THE COMMAND -	EXEC 1.FLEXGEN.TXT
4) WHEN THE PROCESS IS COMPLETE YOU WILL HAVE A NEW
	SYSTEM DISK IN DRIVE 1.


GENERAL INFORMATION

1) THESE DRIVERS HAVE BEEN TESTED WITH TSC FLEX VERSION 9.1
	FOR THE SWTPC COMPUTER, OTHER VERSIONS OF FLEX MAY NOT WORK
	PROPERLY WITH THESE DRIVERS.

2) THESE DRIVERS WILL WORK WITH EITHER A PT-69 COMPUTER SYSTEM
	OR A SWTPC COMPUTER WITH A FD-2 CONTROLLER BOARD.

3) THE DISK DRIVERS HAVE BEEN ASSEMBLED WITH A 6MS STEP RATE.
	IF YOU HAVE A SHUGART SA-400 OR SIMILAR DRIVE YOU WILL NEED
	TO MODIFY THE STEP RATE IN THE DISK DRIVERS AND RE-ASSEMBLE
	THEM. REFER TO THE WD279X DATA SHEET FOR AVAILABLE STEP RATES.

4) THE FD-2 SHOULD BE CONFIGURED FOR 6800 DRIVE READY SELECT

5) FLEX IS A TRADEMARK OF TECHNICAL SYSTEMS CONSULTANTS.
