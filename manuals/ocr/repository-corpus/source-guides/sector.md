.CE
SECTOR
.SP 1
THE SECTOR COMMAND ALLOWS ANY SECTOR ON A DISK TO BE EXAMINED.
THE INFORMATION IS OUTPUT IN BOTH HEX AND ASCII CHARACTERS.
THE UTILITY CAN BE USED TO LOOK AT THE CONTENTS OF THE LOADER AND DIRETORY SECTORS OF TRACK 00 AS WELL AS THE NORMAL DATA SECTORS.
THE INFORMATION GAINED CAN BE USED WITH SECTOR1.CMD TO RECOVER DELETED FILES OR FILES ON A DISK WITH A DAMAGED DIRECTORY.
.SP 2
DESCRIPTION
.SP 1
THE GENERAL SYNTAX OF THE SECTOR UTILITY IS:
.SP 2
.LM 10
SECTOR,(DRIVE),(TRACK),(SECTOR)
.SP 2
.LM 0
WHERE (DRIVE) IS THE DRIVE NUMBER (0-3), (TRACK) IS THE TRACK NUMBER
IN	HEX (00-4C), AND (SECTOR) IS THE SECTOR NUMBER IN HEX (NB. IN FLEX SECTORS START AT 1).
.SP 2
BEFORE THE SECTOR IS DISPLAYED, TWO, 2 DIGIT NUMBERS ARE OUTPUT. THE FIRST
IS THE HEX VALUE OF THE TRACK NUMBER AND THE SECOND IS THE SECTOR NUMBER (ALSO IN HEX).
EACH OF THE LINES OF DATA WILL CONTAIN 16 HEX DIGITS REPRESENTING THE DATA
FOLLOWED BY THE ASCII REPRESENTATIONS OF THE DATA.
ALL NON-PRINTABLE CHARACTERS ARE DISPLAYED AS UNDERSCORES.
.SP 2
EXAMPLE
.SP 2
.LM 10
+++SECTOR 1,3,5
.SP 2
.LM 0
THE ABOVE EXAMPLE WILL PRINT SECTOR 5 OF TRACK 3 FROM THE DISK MOUNTED IN DRIVE 1
.SP 1
