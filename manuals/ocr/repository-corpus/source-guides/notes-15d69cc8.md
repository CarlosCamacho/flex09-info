Special note:-
	The Flex section of the library will I hope be available for at least 1992 but after that check by phone first (0223) 277335. I will also try to assist with any MB 2 enquiries.

	The FLEX section library discs unless stated otherwise are of less than 390 sectors each. Discs Nos 8/9, 12/13, 15/16 are short and each pair count as one disc for charging.
	They are available in Flex format 40 or 80 track single or double sided 5 1/4 inch discs.
	They are also available in 3.5 inch 80T SS or DS Single density
	The charge is #1.00 per library disc and you supply adequate ready formatted discs in reusable packing for the return trip.
	Alternatively send in a disc of good noncopyright programmes suitable for the library and it will be returned with your choice of library disc on it in exchange.
	When submitting programmes please	make comments in the comment field of source code so that members who have different systems can configure it to suit their system and a description of the programme in its header or in a 'readme'.
