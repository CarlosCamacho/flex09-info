USAGE:	The sytax of this command is:
	COPYNEW 1

where "1" is the drive no.

A PREVIOUSLY INITIALISED disk is placed in drive 1 and

the COPYNEW utility in drive 0.

The utility will re-initialise this disk and then

copy all files from the system drive to this new disk.

This new disk will take the name and volume no. of the

disk in drive 0.
