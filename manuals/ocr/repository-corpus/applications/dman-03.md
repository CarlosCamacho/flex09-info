It has been said that competition in the 1980's will not be so much between products and services,
as it will be between management teams.
This holds true for the companies I work for as well as yours.
Information and information management will be one of the important tools of the successful management teams.
The computer is the ideal choice for dealing with large amounts of information because of its speed and accuracy.
But the ability to access this information by management in the form management needs is still largely in the control of
system programmers.
This fact alone with the high cost of computers have kept the companies with gross sales of between
$100,000 and $1,000,000, for the most part, out of the computer market.
But now with the advent of true business computers at affordable prices, companies of this size can now afford
the hardware.
What is now needed is software that can be run by office staff to carry out data input and operations.


Data management programs are not new and there are some computers that are dedicated to data management on a firmware
basis.
Data management programs for micro computers open up the world of information management to users
who could not afford mini or mainframe computers.
The 6800/6809 microcomputer is an outstanding computer that is well within the budget of many small
companies and in multi-tasking configuration, it's a natural for
data base management.


Before attempting a data management program I surveyed as many similar programs as possible.
I looked at information and manuals from many 8080 programs and some large machine programs.
The literature available for most of these programs tell you little if anything about what they will do.
In fact most literature for both micro computer hardware and software is still directed to the 'hobbyest' and
not to the businessman.
It is little wonder that micro computers are finding the business market difficult to penetrate.


My goal was to put together a database management program that could be used either by itself or as a base for a complete business
system.	I call the program 'DATAMAN' and it has taken me more than 2 years to get to this release version.
This set of programs is in use at
the company I work for as well as two other business sites.
DATAMAN is being used to manage a Marketing System for the marketing department, an Order Entry/Billing System
for accounting, as well as many smaller databases for personal and business use.
