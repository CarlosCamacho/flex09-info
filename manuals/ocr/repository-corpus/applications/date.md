# DATE

> Source: `apps/stylograph/PT69-HD - Stylograph Word Processor - Bootable Hard-Disk FLEX System and Utilities.zip!PT69-HD.DSK!DATE.DOC`  
> Method: FLEX disk extraction

OCR and media-decoding errors may remain.

* Accepts Day, Month and Year in any order.
* Day can be 1 or 2 digits, Year is 4 digits
* and Month is ASCII (3 characters minimum).
*
* The following are acceptable inputs:
*
* Feb 15, 2000
*
* 15 February 2000
*
* 2000, 15, Feb
