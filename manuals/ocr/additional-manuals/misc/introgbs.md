I N T R O D U C T I O N


THE GENERAL BUSINESS SYSTEM IS A COMPLETE PROGRAMMING PACKAGE
	FOR THE SMALL BUSINESS ENTERPRISE ENGAGED IN THE SELLING AND
	SERVICING OF MERCHANDISE TO REGULAR CUSTOMERS.	IT IS THE INTENT
	OF THIS PACKAGE TO PROVIDE THE ESSENTIAL INFORMATION FOR MANAGEMENT
	TO PROPERLY EVALUATE THEIR OPERATION, AND TO AID AND ASSIST THEM
	IN MAKING DECISIONS.


THIS IS NOT A SCALED DOWN VERSION OF A LARGE CORPORATION'S
	PROGRAM, BUT A NEW CONCEPT BUILT FROM THE GROUND UP, BY
	CAREFULLY ANALYZING THE REQUIREMENTS OF SMALLER ENTERPRISES
	AND INCORPORATING THESE FINDING INTO ROUTINES WHICH INTERACT
	WITH EACH OTHER TO FORM A COMPLETE PACKAGE.


WE HAVE TRIED VERY HARD TO FOLLOW STANDARD ACCOUNTING
	AND BUSINESS PROCEDURES, USING TERMS, PHRASES, AND PROCEDURES
	THAT ARE FAMILIAR TO THE PERSONEL EMPLOYED BY SMALL BUSINESSES.
	THE MENUS, PROMPTS AND SPECIAL KEYBOARD COMMANDS ARE ALL
	DESIGNED TO LEAD THE OPERATOR DOWN THE RIGHT PATH TO CORRECT
	AND EFFICIENT INPUT.	IT IS VERY EASY TO CORRECT INCORRECT
	INPUT EITHER AS IT IS BEING ENTERED OR LATER WHEN A BALANCE
	DOES NOT AGREE.


IN ESSENCE THE COMPUTER HAS BEEN PROGRAMED TO SPEAK YOUR
	LANGUAGE, FOLLOW YOUR PROCEDURES, AND PRODUCE REPORTS THAT
	YOU AND YOUR PROFESSIONAL ADVISORS CAN UNDERSTAND AND UTILIZE.
	IT IS NOT NECESSARY THAT YOU UNDERSTAND COMPUTERS OR COMPUTER
	PROGRAMING TO EFFECTIVELY UTILIZE THIS PACKAGE.	IT IS NECESSARY
	THAT YOU AND YOUR OPERATORS CAN TYPE, UNDERSTAND BOOKKEEPING
	AND GENERAL OFFICE PROTOCOL.


THE PERMANENT RECORDS PRODUCED BY THE COMPUTER ARE STORED
	ON PAPER AND NOT ON THE FLOPPY DISKS. THIS IS DONE FOR TWO
	REASONS; FIRST WE WOULD VERY QUICKLY RUN OUT OF SPACE AND HAVE
	TO HAVE MORE AND BIGGER DISK DRIVES, SECOND IF THE COMPUTER
	FAILS TO FUNCTION YOU CAN STILL RUN YOU BUSINESS.	AT ANY TIME
	YOU CAN CONVERT BACK TO A MANUAL BOOKKEEPING SYSTEM SO THAT
	YOU ARE NOT DEPENDENT UPON THE COMPUTER FOR THE SUCCESSFUL
	OPERATION OF YOUR BUSINESS.


THE SUCCESSFUL IMPLEMENTATION OF A COMPUTER TO YOUR BUSINESS
	WILL NOT BE AN EASY TASK; SERIOUS PLANNING AND MANY DECISIONS
	MUST BE MADE.	THE PROPER CHOICE AND ALLOCATION OF STOCK NUMBERS
	IS PROBABLY THE MOST IMPORTANT AREA, MISTAKES MADE HERE WILL
	STAY WITH YOU FOR YEARS.	THE SEPARATION OF YOUR OPERATIONS INTO
	DIFFERENT REPORTING GROUPS WILL ALLOW YOU TO HAVE DAILY REPORTS
	ON EACH GROUP, SHOWING PROBLEMS AREA LONG BEFORE THEY BECOME
	SERIOUS.	YOU ARE THE MOST KNOWLEDGEABLE PERSON IN YOUR OPERATION
	AND IT SHOULD BE YOUR DECISIONS WHICH ADAPT THE BUSINESS AND
	COMPUTER TO A HARMONIOUS UNION.


NO COMPUTER CAN PRODUCE USEFUL OUTPUT UNTIL THE INVENTORY,
	CUSTOMERS, VENDORS, ETC ARE ENTERED INTO THE DATA BASE. YOU
	MUST COMMIT THE RESOURCES TO GET THIS JOB DONE AND DONE
	QUICKLY.	EVERY EMPLOYEE MUST FULLY UNDERSTAND THAT THE
	ACCURACY AND UTILITY OF THE COMPUTER IS DEPENDENT UPON GOOD
	INPUT AND THAT ALL TRANSACTIONS MUST BE REPORTED.


THE COMPUTER AND THIS PROGRAM PACKAGE WILL BE A LOYAL AND
	DEPENDABLE TOOL FOR YOU TO USE IN THE MANAGEMENT OF YOUR BUSINESS.
	DO NOT EXPECT THAT IT WILL SAVE YOU MONEY, FOR IT WILL TAKE A LOT
	OF TIME AND EFFORT TO GET STARTED, AND A CONTINUING EXPENSE TO
	MAINTAIN THE DATA BASE ACCURATE AND UP TO DATE.	HOWEVER; IF THE
	DATA BASE IS ACCURATE AND TIMELY YOU WILL RECEIVE VALUABLE INFOR-
	MATION CONCERNING ALL PHASES OF YOUR OPERATION WHICH WILL ASSIST
	YOU IN MAKING DECISIONS THAT WILL MAKE MONEY.


STUDY THESE INSTRUCTIONS CAREFULLY, MAKE OR SECURE COPIES FOR
	ALL YOUR KEY PERSONEL AND OPERATORS.	ENCOURAGE THEM TO STUDY IT
	AND HOLD DISCUSSIONS GROUPS. LOCATE ANOTHER COMPANY WHICH HAS
	ALREADY IMPLEMENTED THIS PACKAGE AND ARRANGE FOR YOUR PERSONEL
	TO MEET THEIR COUNTERPARTS AND IF POSSIBLE TO SPEND SOME TIME
	WATCHING THEM USE THE COMPUTER.
