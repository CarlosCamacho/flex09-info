POWERING COMPUTER UP AND DOWN

NEVER POWER THE MACHINE UP OR DOWN WITH DISKS IN THE DRIVE SLOTS.

TO POWER UP:
	1.	TURN MAIN POWER SWITCH TO "ON" POSITION
	2.	INSERT DISKS INTO DRIVE SLOTS
		A.	MASTER DISK OR PROGRAM DISK TO DRIVE 0
		B.	SYSTEM DISK, BLANK DISK OR DATA DISK TO DRIVE 1
	3.	CLOSE DOORS TO DRIVE SLOTS.
	4.	A $ AND A CURSON ( ) SHOULD APPEAR IN THE TOP LEFT
		HAND CORNER OF YOUR SCREEN.
	5.	IF THEY DO NOT APPEAR, OPEN THE DOORS AND THEN PRESS
		THE "RESET" BUTTON.	DO NOT RESET WITH THE DRIVE DOORS
		CLOSED.
	6.	RECLOSE THE DRIVE DOORS
	7.	YOU ARE NOW READY TO PROCEED TO WHATEVER FUNCTION YOU
		HAVE CHOSEN TO PERFORM.
	

TO POWER DOWN:
	1.	WHEN YOU HAVE COMPLETED ALL WORK WITH THE COMPUTER,
		YOU ARE NOW READY TO POWER THE COMPUTER DOWN.
	2.	REMOVE ALL DISKS FROM THE DRIVE SLOTS AND FILE THESE
		IN THEIR APPROPRIATE PLACES.
	3.	WITTH THE DRIVE DOORS OPEN, SIMPLY TURN YOUR MAIN POWER
		SWITCH TO THE "OFF" POSITION.

NOTE:	FOR MACHINES NOT EQUIPPED WITH A CENTRALIZED POWER SWITCH
	YOU SHOULD TURN THE POWER SWITCHES TO THE ON POSITION WHICH
	ARE LOCATED ON THE FRONT OF THE COMPUTERS MAIN-FRAME, ON
	THE REAR OF THE TERMINAL, AND ON THE FRONT OF THE PRINTER.
	THE NOTE ON THE "RESET" STILL APPLIES.
