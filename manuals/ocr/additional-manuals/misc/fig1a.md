 This  is  an  illustration  of the 'window editing' concept. As you can see, the
 document  is  a  large  scroll  behind  the  editing  window.  The   window   is
 representative  of  your  terminal's  screen.  Moving  the window up and down or
 rolling the scroll behind the window, and moving the window or the  scroll  left
 or right lets you see all of your text a window at a time.

 The  sign  over  the  window  is  representative of special information that the
 window editor presents to you on the screen about where you are in the scroll.
