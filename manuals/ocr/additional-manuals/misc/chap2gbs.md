	I N V O I C E I N G

		AN INVOICE IS USUALLY PREPARED FROM SOME PEICE OF PAPER
	CALLED A SALES SLIP, SERVICE ORDER, OR OTHER DESIGNATION.
	THERE ARE 7 DIFFERENT ROUTINES WHICH PRODUCE INVOICES, AND
	WE WILL DESCRIBE THEN ONE AT A TIME.	FROM THE MENU YOU MUST
	SELECT "TRANSACTION ENTRY" WHERE YOU THEN CAN CHOOSE.

	1. - GENERATE INVOICE

		YOU WILL BE ASKED IF THE PROPER DISK IS INSTALLED AND
	THE INVOICE MASK WILL BE DISPLAYED.	THE FIRST REQUEST
	IS FOR THE SALES ACCOUNT NUMBER IF YOU DO NOT USE THIS
	FEATURE HIT C/R AND CONTINUE.	THE SALES ACCT # IS
	A 1 DIGIT NUMBER AND ONLY THE LAST DIGITS ENTERED WILL
	BE USED.	NEXT YOU WILL BE ASKED IF YOU WISH TO ENTER FROM A
	DELIVERY ORDER, ANSWER (Y OR N).	DELIVERY ORDERS WILL BE
	COVERED LATER.

		THE CUSTOMER NUMBER IS NOW REQUESTED AND THE NUMBER
	ENTERED WILL BE CHECKED FOR VALIDITY, ANY DISCREPANCY WILL
	BE REPORTED ON THE BULLETIN LINE AND ANOTHER CUSTOMER NUMBER
	WILL BE REQUESTED.	WHEN THE COMPUTER ACCEPTS THE CUSTOMER
	NUMBER IT WILL THEN DISPLAY IN THE MASK THE CUST #, TAX CODE,
	CUSTOMER NAME & ADDRESS, THE EFFECTIVE DATE, AND INVOICE #.
	THE NEXT REQUEST IS FOR ORDER #, THIS IS THE NUMBER ON THE
	SALES SLIP, ETC	IF YOU ARE USING THIS FEATURE ENTER THE
	NUMBER (6 DIGITS ALLOWED) IF NOT ENTER C/R TO IGNORE.

		A MAXIMUN OF 10 ITEMS CAN BE ENTERED ON ONE INVOICE
	AND THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURE WILL BE REPEATED UNTIL 10 ITEMS
	ARE ENTERED OR YOU ENTER THE QUANTITY OF ZERO.	THE MAX QUANTITY
	PERMITTED IS 7999 AND WHEN REQUESTED ENTER THE NUMBER OF UNITS
	SOLD FOR THIS ITEM.	THE NEXT REQUEST IS FOR THE STOCK NUMBER
	AND AGAIN THIS IS CHECKED FOR VALIDITY AND ANY DESCREPANCY IS
	REPORTED.	A VALID NUMBER MUST BE ENTERED TO CONTINUE AND IN THE
	EVENT THIS IS NOT POSSIBLE THE "ABORT" KEY WILL RETURN TO THE
	MONITOR.	AFTER THE STOCK # IS ACCEPTED YOU HAVE THE OPPORTUNITY
	TO ENTER A 20 CHAR REMARK, USE C/R TO SKIP IT.	NOW THE COMPUTER
	WILL DISPLAY THE QUANTITY, STOCK #, REMARK, LIST PRICE, AND
	EXTENSION IN THE MASK AND THEN REQUEST THE QUANTITY FOR THE NEXT
	ITEM.	CONTINUE ENTERING UNTIL THE INVOICE IS FINISHED,	WHEN 10
	ITEMS OR A QUANTITY OF ZERO HAS BEEN ENTERED THE COMPUTER WILL
	TOTAL THE EXTENSIONS, CALCULATE THE SALES TAX, AND PRODUCE
	THE GRAND TOTAL.	THE (*) PROMPT WILL NOW BE DISPLAYED ON THE
	COMMAND LINE AND ALL THE SPECIAL KEYS ACTIVATED.	YOU MAY
	"ADD" MORE ITEMS, "TEST" STOCK LEVELS, CHANGE ENTRIES ON THE
	SCREEN AND "REPL" THE RECORD WITH THE EDITED SCREEN RECORD,
	OR YOU CAN "DELET" AND START OVER.	IF EVERYTHING IS CORRECT
	YOU CAN "PRINT" THE INVOICE AT WHICH TIME THE INVOICE IS
	INSTALLED INTO THE DAILY JOURNAL AND THE STOCK LEVELS ARE
	ADJUSTED.	IN THE EVENT THE PRINTER DID NOT FUNCTION CORRECTLY
	YOU HAVE THE OPPORTUNITY TO PRINT THE INVOICE OVER.	TO
	ENTER THE NEXT INVOICE USE THE "DELET" KEY TO CLEAR THE
	SCREEN AND START OVER.
