The object of this puzzle is to change the patterns
of 0's and 1's until the board has a 0 in the center
and 1's in all other positions.
	To change the board pattern, enter the
number of a square that contains a 1.	Enter the
squares position number as follows:

	1	2	3
	4	5	6
	7	8	9

	Choosing a square in the center of an edge
(2,4,6,8) causes all positions along the edge to
change state.	(0's become 1's and 1's become 0's).

	Choosing a corner square (1,3,7,9) causes
the corner square and the three adjacent square to
change state.

	Finally, if you choose the center square (5),
all but the corner squares will change state.

	To end the game, hit <delete>.
