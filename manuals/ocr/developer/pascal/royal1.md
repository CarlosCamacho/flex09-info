Royality structure for all programs.	June 1981

	In order to make it a profitable situtation for all concerned this will be the royality breakdown for the forseeable future. I believe this to be a very fair split.

	There are several costs involved in producing software. I have broken them down into 3 parts.

	1)	HARD costs. These are the actual costs to
		produce the product.

	2)	MANUAL costs. The cost to print the manual
		and the binder if any.

	3)	Advertizing costs. The cost of the advertizment itself and the
		cost of artwork.

	The HARD costs include all the things needed to actually send the product out. ( Disks, paperwork, copy time, bookkepping, etc.) We have set this cost at $10.00 for (1) one 5 or 8 inch disk with a additional $3.00 for programs with (2) two 5" disks.

	The MANUAL is a variable and is our actual cost of producing this.

	The ADVERTIZING is another variable that will change depending on how large a ad. We will split the costs of this part. The artwork is the layout of the ad. This is a onetime cost and is taken off the top of the first months revinue. Any artwork done later ( small changes etc ) is paid for by us. A complete rework would be taken off the top again.

	For example: If you had a $50.00 program on one disk and you sold 10 of them in a month. The ad size for that month was 1/8 page ( see new ad layout enclosed ) and the manual cost was $2.00. This is how it would brakdown.

+	$500.00	Gross income. ( 50x10=500 )
-	100.00	HARD costs ( 10x10=100 )
-	20.00	MANUAL costs ( 2x10=20 )
-	25.00	One time layout charge ( varies )
	--------
	$355.00	Net income after fixed expences.
-	25.00	1/2 the cost of advertizing.
	--------
	$305.00	Net income after all expences.
	/	2	divide by 2 for royality
	--------
	$152.50	Royality payment

	For your part you are expected to provide the program working to the specs with no bugs. When there are bugs found you are expected to fix them promptly. You are also expected to provide the manual ready to print on disk in text processor format. Either TSC's or STYLUS format.
	For our part we will provide consultation on the programs, advise as to changes, bookeeping, advertizing, produce the manuals, disk(s), mailings, and 1 line in the catalog in the magazine section of the adv. With each royality payment (usually by the middle of the month following) you will recieve a list of the purchasers and copies of the adv.
